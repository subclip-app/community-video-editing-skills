# Style recipes

Choose for purpose, mood, formality, and density. Compare different compositions as well as different colors. Carry the selected type roles, palette, spacing, and decorative device through title, evidence, comparison, quote, and closing slides. Use real content for any visible labels.

## Bold options

- **Signal** suits formal reporting. Place one high-contrast serif claim on deep navy with bone paper and muted gold. Use gold rules to organize sections. For evidence slides use a paper panel, slim chart lines, and a restrained sans for labels.
- **Grove** suits considered, patient subjects. Use forest #192B1B, cream #D4CFBF, and terracotta #C8524A. Set headlines and figures in regular Playfair Display with short italic accent phrases. Pair Jost body with narrow mono labels. Extend the same hairline rules, generous margins, and short accent mark to quotes and charts.
- **Studio** suits an energetic launch. Use a near-black canvas and electric-yellow display type with one dominant statement. Carry the yellow to a single focal number or chart mark. Keep fine body text high contrast and use the same edges to align every slide.
- **Retro Zine** suits a tactile editorial story. Use khaki #C8B99A, forest #008F4D, ink #1A1A1A, and pale paper #F4EFE6. Pair condensed Bebas Neue display with Space Grotesk body and Caveat accents. Extend off-center stamps, paper offsets, and restrained grain into quotes and evidence spreads.
- **Capsule** suits a playful showcase. Use bone #F5F5F0 and ink #1A1A1A with coral, lavender, lime, or sky. Set Bodoni Moda display with Space Grotesk labels. Repeat outlined pill geometry and restrained offsets in diagrams and comparison rows.

For CJK content, verify glyph coverage, use suitable verified serif or sans companions, give lines room, and avoid Latin-style tracking on CJK runs. For a custom wildcard, choose a clear visual thesis and one graphic device that can extend to several layouts. Keep the palette deliberate and graphic effects subordinate to the content.

## Motion and fit

- **Professional:** A quick 200 to 300 ms rise or fade, then a stable reading hold.
- **Dramatic:** An unhurried fade or slow scale from 0.9 to 1 on a focal graphic, with copy settled by its cue.
- **Editorial:** Sequence a headline, image, and pull quote at their beats. Hold the complete composition for reading.
- **Playful:** Use one brief overshoot on a geometric accent. Keep small text steady.
- **Quiet:** Use a soft fade and steady frame with generous negative space.
- **Technical:** Reveal a grid or trace a path as the concept is introduced. Keep the type sharply legible.

Use opacity and transforms for text and its ancestors. Reserve filters for isolated decoration when verified in export. Translate the original viewport proportions into fixed pixels and keep the whole composition inside the stage. Read the settled frame at the intended viewing size. If a slide needs smaller type, scrolling, or collisions to fit, split it or change the layout.

For a concrete brief, use this pattern: “A speaker-led lesson with one point per slide. Build the opening around the actual lesson title and one graphic from its subject. Compare Swiss Modern, Grove, and a subject-specific wildcard. Carry the selected heading, colors, and margins into one example slide and one takeaway. Reveal evidence when the voice calls it out and hold the final text long enough to read.”
