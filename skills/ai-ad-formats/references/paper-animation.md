# Paper animation ad

Look: scenes built from paper with visible layers and soft shadows. The five styles below are suggestions.

| Sub-style | Feel |
|---|---|
| Layered papercraft | Stacked cut layers with depth |
| Origami | Folded shapes that unfold into the product |
| Torn collage | Rough edges, mixed textures |
| Pop-up book | Scenes that fold up from a page |
| Cut-out puppets | Flat jointed characters moving on a paper stage |

## Beats (6)

1. Hook: a folded shape unfolds, or a page opens.
2. World: the paper set builds.
3. Product arrives in paper form, then reveals the real product photo.
4. Benefit shown by a simple paper action.
5. Payoff: the set settles into a finished scene.
6. End card with native text.

Prompt line: `Paper craft scene, <sub-style>, layered paper pieces with soft shadows, visible fiber texture, fixed camera. The product matches the attached photo exactly.`

For a pure collage build use the `paper-cut-motion` skill.
