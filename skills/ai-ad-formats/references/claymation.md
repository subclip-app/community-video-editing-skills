# Claymation ad

Look: handmade clay, fingerprints, slight wobble, stop motion at about 12 frames a second.

## Pick a sub-style

| Sub-style | Feel |
|---|---|
| Goofy clay | Bright, chunky, big eyes, silly gags |
| Soft pastel | Gentle, kid friendly, round shapes |
| Dark comedy clay | Deadpan, moody light, dry jokes |
| Premium studio plasticine | Clean, polished, commercial lighting |

Show the creator a small menu of these and let them choose, or choose the one that fits the brand.

## Beats (7)

1. Hook: the product in clay, one surprising gesture.
2. Problem: a clay character has the problem.
3. Product enters.
4. Use: a visible, simple action.
5. Change: the problem goes away in a funny way.
6. Proof or payoff shot.
7. End card: the real product photo with native text.

## Prompt line (paste in every picture request)

`Handmade claymation, <sub-style>, visible fingerprints, soft studio light, shallow depth of field, stop motion look. The product matches the attached photo exactly.`

Video: ask for slightly stepped motion and a short single action per clip.
