# Twelve illustration styles

## The wrapper (use for every style)

```text
Transform the provided photo into [STYLE NAME]. Keep the framing, camera angle, pose, gaze,
facial expression and hand gestures of every person, but freely redesign faces, bodies,
clothing, objects and surroundings. The style matters far more than likeness.

CHARACTERS: [from the table]
SURROUNDINGS: [from the table]
PALETTE AND SURFACE: [from the table]
Faces, hands, clothes, objects and background must all follow the same visual language.

Keep the aspect ratio of the source photo. No [ban list from the table], no text, no logos.
```

## The styles

| # | Style | Characters | Surroundings | Palette and surface | Ban |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cartoon Modern (1950s UPA TV cartoon) | Asymmetric silhouettes: wedge head, long narrow neck, sharp triangular nose, tiny oval eyes, angular hair, small mouth; hands from a few angular shapes; oversized suit jacket, narrow shirt, thin tie; break proportion and symmetry. | Large irregular geometric planes, atomic starbursts, off-centre lamps, boomerang shapes; every object a flat silhouette. | Mustard yellow, muted turquoise, coral red, charcoal, warm ivory; crisp contours of uneven weight, minimal hard shadows, awkward perspective, aged cel paint. | normal anatomy, detailed eyes, painterly shading, gradients, 3D |
| 2 | Graphic noir (1940s detective) | Dark fedora, oversized trench coat, white shirt, loose tie; faces and hands as a few cream-lit shapes swallowed by black; brim shadows across the eyes. | Tilted blinds throwing long horizontal shadow stripes, black skyline, a lamp glow, deep pools of dark. | Almost only matte black, warm ivory and saturated golden yellow; poster-like shadow shapes, hard slashes of light, restrained paper texture. | photographic faces, smooth cinematic gradients, extra colours, 3D |
| 3 | Superflat (Japanese pop art) | Rounded charming characters with big bright eyes, simple features, clean dark outlines, thick sculpted hair, joyful faces, clothes covered in big flowers. | Oversized smiling flowers at several scales, flat clouds, a bold rainbow arc; faces and hands stay clear. | Opaque blocks of royal blue, hot pink, sunshine yellow, turquoise, white, navy outlines; almost no shading or depth. | realistic skin, shadows, photographic objects, 3D |
| 4 | Caricature (hand-drawn) | Long narrow head, oversized crooked nose, tiny intense eyes, big uneven ears, hollow cheeks, jutting chin, thin neck on sloping shoulders; knobbly fingers. Witty and human, never frightening. | Crooked: tilted windows, bent shelves, sagging curtains; only a few large background shapes. | Sulfur yellow, faded brick red, olive green, smoky teal, dark brown; scratchy pen lines, sparse crosshatching, uneven painted fills. | realistic proportions, polished storybook painting, clean vector geometry, horror |
| 5 | Risograph zine | Lively graphic characters: simple faces, few strong lines for hair and brows, boxy clothes with oversized lapels; clear silhouettes at small size. | Energetic zine composition: oversized circles, off-centre blocks, starbursts, paper cutouts. | Three fluorescent inks only (hot pink, cobalt blue, sunflower yellow) on warm off-white paper; extra colours only where inks overlap; coarse halftone screens, porous paper, small misregistration at edges. | photographic faces, full-colour digital shading, gradients, glossy paper |
| 6 | Neon punk anime | Large expressive eyes, sharp simple nose, bold pointed hair locks, dark oversized streetwear with high collars, cyan seams, magenta accents, patches without lettering. | A rain-soaked futuristic night city: glowing panels, cables, blurred neon; darker areas around faces. | Deep navy, electric cyan, fuchsia, violet, a little acid green; confident line art, crisp cel shading in two or three tones, cyan and pink rim light. | photographic faces, airbrushed skin, realistic 3D lighting, armour suits, readable neon lettering |
| 7 | Ligne claire (European adventure comic) | Simple rounded faces, small eyes, minimal nose, hair as a few curved shapes, bright adventure clothes (blue shirt, red neckerchief). | An orderly comic world: European street, facades, lamps, rooftops, clouds, drawn with the same outlines as the figures. | Smooth black outlines of nearly uniform thickness; solid flat fills in sky blue, cream, brick red, yellow, muted green; consistent perspective. | realistic shading, brushstrokes, halftone, crosshatching, dramatic shadows, speech bubbles |
| 8 | Gouache picture book | Slightly oversized heads, simplified features, expressive eyes, broad hands from a few strokes; faces built from four or five opaque colour areas; loose clothes as irregular matte patches. | Big simple window shapes, oversized plants with silhouette leaves, furniture reduced to essentials. | Terracotta, butter yellow, dusty blue, sage green, cream, deep plum; thick opaque matte paint, bristle marks, dry-brush streaks, visible paper; colours separated by painted shapes, not outlines. | realistic faces, fine detail, transparent watercolour, clean vector shapes |
| 9 | Constructivism (1920s poster) | Monumental figures from angular silhouettes and hard shadow planes; faces as black, cream and red shapes with expressive eyes and mouth; sharp-collared work jackets; broad shoulders. | A huge red circle behind the main figure, black diagonal beams, cream triangular cutouts, a few industrial shapes at the edges. | Vermilion, charcoal, warm cream, a little grey; flat stencil colour, slight ink wear, rough printed edges; energy from scale and geometry. | photographic faces, painterly shading, halftone, 3D, any letters |
| 10 | Neo-expressionism (raw flat paint) | Faces as flat painted masks of three to five raw patches of unmixed colour with thick black brush lines for features; crude uneven heads; hands as flat shapes. | Flat blocks of violent colour, crooked wall shapes, scratchy black gestural marks. | Cobalt blue, vermilion, dirty yellow, bone white, black, as unblended paint with brush drags, knife scrapes, drips and charcoal scribbles; colour spills over outlines; completely flat, no volume or depth. | realistic faces, soft shading, gradients, depth of field, polished digital painting |
| 11 | Persian miniature | Graceful elongated figures with delicate oval faces, almond eyes, fine arched brows; flowing silk robes with floral embroidery, ornate cap or wrapped turban. | A jewel-toned palace garden: pointed tiled arches, patterned walls, flowering branches, slender cypresses, small birds. | Opaque fields of lapis blue, turquoise, vermilion, emerald, ivory, luminous gold; fine floral and geometric patterns, delicate ink contours; shallow stacked planes, flattened perspective, a narrow illuminated border, aged paper. | photographic skin, realistic light, depth of field, Western perspective, readable script |
| 12 | Cel-shaded mecha | Each person as a pilot in a large humanoid combat suit: massive angular shoulders, segmented chest plate, mechanical forearms, articulated metal hands keeping the gestures; an open helmet so the face stays visible. | A towering hangar with big panels, beams, maintenance lights and the partial silhouette of another giant robot. | White armour, deep navy joints, vermilion accents, safety yellow, graphite shadow; bold panel lines, large bevelled armour shapes, hard two-tone cel shading. | plain flight suit, realistic metal reflections, soft 3D rendering, closed helmet, lettering |

## Picking a style

- Thumbnails and strong faces: Cartoon Modern, Superflat, Neon punk anime.
- Moody or serious: Graphic noir, Neo-expressionism, Constructivism.
- Warm or editorial: Gouache, Ligne claire, Risograph.
- Ornamental and rich: Persian miniature.
- Funny: Caricature.
