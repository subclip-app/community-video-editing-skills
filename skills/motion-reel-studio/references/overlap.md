# Overlap with other community skills

| Skill | What it covers | How this skill differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `code-motion-styles` | 16 short code-built style prompts (3D charts, glow sketch, cel anime launch, brick world, particle galaxy, glitch cut) | Short pieces and backdrops. This skill is a whole vertical reel with a script, a voice, word-keyed scenes and delivery, in five named styles. Its anime style is a full 24 second episode, not a launch card. |
| `code-animation-styles-remotion` | 39 code-drawn looks as layers | A look catalog. Use it when only a look is needed. |
| `hyperframes-motion-layers`, `remotion-motion-layers` | The layer engines and their contracts | This skill builds on them. It adds the five styles, the script and voice pipeline, the safe zone and loudness delivery. |
| `threejs-3d-scenes`, `r3f-physics-and-effects` | 3D scene craft in layers | Use them for the 3D build of styles 02, 03 and 04. |
| `motion-polish`, `video-shotcraft` | Motion quality and shot craft | Applied in the checks here; read them for deeper craft. |
| `tvhead-story-looks` | Narrated character stories in 15 looks (the other half of the same upstream) | Stories with characters and a storyboard. This skill is promo and list reels with mascots. |
| `trendjack-animator` | Trending animated ad formats | Ads. This skill is reels in code. |
| `blender-to-subclip` | Blender renders into Subclip | The upstream's `blender-3d` skill needs Blender; use this skill for plates. |

Not adapted from the upstream: `ai-montage` (documentary montage from real data and archive photos; see `vox-explainer` and `documentary-case-file`) and `blender-3d` (needs Blender 5.2). They are related upstream skills only.
