# Overlap with other community skills

| Skill | What it covers | How this skill differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cartoon-episodes` | A show bible and consistent AI cartoon clips cut into episodes | This skill is one-off narrated stories in 15 named looks, with a director's proposal and prompt packages. For recurring characters across many episodes use `cartoon-episodes`. |
| `cartoon-animation` | Hand-painted cartoon shots with boiling ink linework | One look among 15 here (doodle and cartoon). Use `cartoon-animation` for painted shot craft. |
| `code-motion-styles` | 16 code-built motion style prompts for short pieces | Short pieces and backdrops. This skill builds a whole narrated story; its Route B layers can borrow styles from there (stickman notebook, brick world, cel anime). |
| `code-animation-styles-remotion` | 39 code-drawn looks | Look catalog without a story engine. Use it when only a look is needed, not a narrated story. |
| `stickman-video-director` | The stickman director and its styles as a standalone skill | Referenced by slug only. This skill includes the five stickman looks as part of its 15 looks plus the proposal and prompt package; for a stickman-only workflow use that skill. |
| `ai-style-atlas` | 86 visual styles as restyle recipes | Styles for any explainer. This skill's looks come with story structure, cast, sets, props, captions and music. |
| `trendjack-animator` | Trending vertical animated ad formats with an audio-first clock | Ads and songs. This skill is explainers and stories; it can use the same plate lock idea. |
| `vox-explainer`, `vox-director` | Vox-style documentary collage explainers | A different genre. Use those for archival collage. |
| `svg-character-animation`, `paper-cut-motion`, `pixelfx` | Code characters, paper cut-out, pixel art | Use their craft for one-off pieces; this skill orchestrates a full story. |

Not adapted from the upstream: `ai-montage` (needs real source material and charts, see `vox-explainer` and `documentary-case-file`) and `blender-3d` (needs Blender; see `blender-to-subclip`). Also not adapted: the upstream's After Effects plates (needs After Effects) and its local studio app.

The upstream's `motion-studio` is adapted as the separate skill `motion-reel-studio`.
