# Writing the story

## What it is for
The script and the picture plan for every look. Read it before writing the story table.

## Script rules
- **Hook in line 1** (12 words or fewer): a contradiction, a confession or a question the viewer has felt.
- **One idea per scene**, 6 to 22 spoken words, short sentences, spoken language, contractions.
- **Arc:** hook, the familiar situation, the hidden mechanism (the why), the cost, the turn (what to do), a quiet last line that echoes the hook.
- **Length:** about 150 spoken words per minute. 60 seconds is 14 to 18 scenes. Default 60 to 90 seconds landscape, 30 to 60 seconds vertical.
- Concrete beats abstract. Use numbers only when real, or phrase them softly.
- Write numbers and symbols as words in the spoken line ("dollars", not a dollar sign).
- Last scene: short, lands on an image, hold it 0.6 seconds.

## Picture rules
- Every scene has a **visual metaphor**, not an illustration of the words: loneliness is one figure in a spotlight; addiction is standing on a pile of TVs holding up an endless feed; burnout is a battery draining; overthinking is a scribble over the head.
- Reuse one hero across the film so it reads as a story.
- Something changes **on the key word** of every line (a face, a pose, a prop).
- Vary framing: wide, close push, dark spotlight, wide again. At most 3 cuts in a row with one layout.
- On-screen text: 1 to 3 words, only the key concept.
- 1 to 4 characters, 0 to 3 props per picture. Empty space is part of the style.

## Plan in blocks
Plan in about 10 second blocks, about 20 to 25 English words each, three beats per block: 0 to 3 s set up or inherit, 3 to 7 s escalate, 7 to 10 s pay off and hand over. Name the connection into the next block (a pose, a prop, a direction or a deliberate location cut). Cozy stories change location every block.

## Engineering formats (looks 14 and 15)
- **Underground sim** (about 45 to 55 seconds, 11 to 12 lines): absurd goal in a hidden room, problem 1, simple fix, problem 2, fix, problem 3, fix, the result on the counter, a side effect of success, its fix or a punchline, a congratulation plus a summary card, then an invitation to comment. Problems turn the HUD red and get a stamp; every fix adds an upgrade chip.
- **Marker sketch** (about 45 to 50 seconds): "Want a [thing]? Let's draw it" while the hand draws the object, each problem in red, the fix in green or blue, one line of real arithmetic beside it, a page flip to a summary (problem struck out, fix ticked, boxed result), then a call to action. Numbers are real arithmetic or honest assumptions written as such.
- Both: show safe, legal engineering only. Skip ideas about evading platform bans, tapping utilities or hiding hazards.
