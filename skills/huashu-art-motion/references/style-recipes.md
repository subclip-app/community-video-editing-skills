# Style recipes

These 53 summaries adapt the upstream recipe cards. IDs retain the source numbering, including its gaps. They describe art direction and implementation ideas, not callable Subclip scenes. None has been frame-tested in this conversion. Earlier source demonstrations are historical evidence only; research recipes remain research in Subclip.

For a new subject, read the frame-first and cross-work study method in [directing](directing.md). Select a specific period or film where relevant. Observe several distinct references before claiming fidelity. Rebuild composition for the new action. Match the character, props and environment to the same medium.

## Earlier source recipes, 01 to 37

| ID and language | Shape and material recipe | Motion and known trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Cave painting | Ochre rock relief, charcoal silhouettes, porous pigment edges and negative hand shapes. Use restrained mid-frequency rock noise. | Two-pose running animals, local firelight and embers. Excess light washes ochre pale; broad noise can look like camouflage. |
| 02 Egyptian mural | Flat plaster, profile face with frontal eye/shoulders, clear brown outlines, registers and restrained mineral colors. No generic painterly brush filter. | Border/water patterns advance, rays articulate, main action stays readable. Avoid over-repeating one glyph or cropping tall headdresses. |
| 03 Greek black-figure | Warm terracotta ground, near-black silhouettes, fine terracotta incised lines, small red/white additions. All three are needed. | Counter-moving meander bands and small profile actions. Keep fine incisions readable and preserve warm clay under the black. |
| 04 Roman mosaic | Fixed tessera layout follows contours, colored faces, narrow grout and relief. Tile half-size slightly larger than half-spacing reduces excessive grout. | Local smoke, moving light and color through fixed tiles. Do not change tile density only where a hand moves or leave thin tails as grout. |
| 05 Gothic gold | Gold-ground texture, embossed marks with tiny light/dark offsets, ornamental pattern and crisp silhouettes. | Restrict moving glints to gold masks. Do not let foil highlights bleach all figures. |
| 06 Renaissance painting | Coherent volume, soft edge transitions, restrained glaze and canvas. Blend a soft image with a clearer detail pass. | Controlled small actions and mechanical motifs. Surface cracks are secondary to shape and light. |
| 08 Impressionism | Broken short colored strokes, cool/warm variation and region-specific palettes. Background and fine figure strokes use different scales. | Stable stroke sites with continuous directional change. Sequential random streams can reshuffle the entire painting when one object moves. |
| 09 Post-impressionist flow | Directional long strokes, regional color families and dark edging; finer isolated props and face details. | Swirling fields and expanding light motifs. Thick global strokes destroy small objects; full-screen seed changes cause flashing. |
| 10 Art Nouveau | Variable-width organic contours, decorative ribbons, flat areas and warm gold/brown linework. | Grow S-curves, vegetation and ribbon steam. Steam must lean away from the face as the prop moves. |
| 11 Cubism | Intersecting planes, partial views, faceted color relationships and deliberate edge breaks. | Animate facets while keeping the event intelligible. A generic triangle mesh alone lacks the spatial idea. |
| 12 Bauhaus | Primary-color geometry, circles, bars, mechanical relationships and clear negative space. | Rotate pointers, offset geometric cycles and reveal structure. Geometry needs a story action, not decorative motion alone. |
| 13 Pop print | Bold flat color, print registration offsets and halftone dots on a stable angled grid. | Step between color separations or panel treatments. Keep identity and readable contrast through changes. |
| 14 8-bit | Draw at a low working resolution such as 240 by 135, use a limited palette of about 14 colors and nearest-neighbor scale. | Discrete pose changes and pixel reveals. Do not smooth edges or add subpixel drift to the pixel art. |
| 15 Early ray-traced CGI | Hard highlights, reflective floors, simple plastic shading and deliberate early-CGI geometry. | Reflections and object motion share geometry. This is a historical aesthetic, not a general recipe for modern character films. |
| 16 Contemporary illustration | Flat designed shapes, controlled grain density, clean silhouette and detailed pose construction. | Anticipation, squash, follow-through and a concrete closing action. Do not import the source's private character assets. |
| 17 Chinese ink | Paper remains the ground; ink, dry-brush gaps, washes and distant pale forms build on it. Keep intentional negative space. | Brush reveals, bamboo, small birds and restrained ink movement. Continuous bristle paths avoid bead-like joints; cache completed strokes. |
| 18 Klimt ornament | Gold structure and repeated geometric decoration within silhouettes, with a readable face and hands. | Local gold glints and ornament movement. Build pattern on one scratch layer, then mask once; repeated destructive masking can erase it. |
| 19 Expressionist flow | Long curving strokes follow the region; controlled line-wise distortion can bend the world. | Continuous waves and curved fields. Overscan by the displacement range to prevent exposed edges. |
| 20 Dunhuang decorative study | Mineral colors, decorative mural bands and long flowing ribbons. This older sample is distinct from 55's period-specific research. | Ribbon motion needs a broad horizontal arc to read as airborne rather than chair rods. |
| 21 Repeated-dot installation | Repetition, coherent dot scale and strong figure/ground separation; reflected lights on an ordered grid. | Controlled local pulses and repetition. Cap dot radius to avoid a white frame; oversized random glows look like bokeh. |
| 22 Constructivist collage | Red/black wedges, diagonals, paper and grayscale photographic halftone. | Radiating text/shape relationships and angular reveals. Use real permitted photos when the content is evidence. |
| 23 Surrealist material | Precise hard-lit space contrasts with soft, sagging objects and long shadows. | Continuous deformation and restrained distant movement. A hanging form needs a rounded spreading underside, not a pointed cone. |
| 24 Hopper-like light | Architectural planes, strong light/shadow blocks and a coherent quiet scene. | Slow light movement, shade pull and dust in actual light. Keep a dark subject readable without flattening the intended isolation. |
| 25 Painted animation background | Select a film-level direction; transparent washes and soft backgrounds can contrast with deliberate cel shapes. | Foliage, cloth and coherent light patches. Generic watercolor is not proof of a studio-wide style. |
| 26 Vaporwave | Deliberate retro electronic palette, channel offsets, scan lines and localized tracking noise. | Restrained VHS-style drift. Use only when this color/effect direction is chosen; do not make it the default palette. |
| 27 Kirby-like comics | Heavy structural ink, forceful foreshortening, flat print color and patterned energy. | Strong silhouettes and perspective action. Clean pop color alone misses the blocky anatomy and physical force. |
| 28 Monet-like light | Distinct short strokes for sky, water and foliage; colored shadows rather than gray shading. | Water reflections, foliage and light evolve locally. Separate a bridge/prop into a fine-detail pass so foliage does not swallow it. |
| 29 Seurat-like pointillism | Similar-sized dots from a controlled pure-color palette, proportioned to mix optically with complementary accents. | Slow object action through stable dot sites. Random short impressionist dabs are a different medium. |
| 30 Matisse-like cutouts | Colored paper, purposeful organic silhouettes, edge variation and intentional layer order. | Cut shapes pivot, slide or peel. Keep falling pieces away from faces and give labels a legible paper backing. |
| 31 Haring-like line | Equal-weight bold contours, clear figures and rhythmic marks. | Poses and surrounding marks carry energy. Fine hair lines and broken joint caps conflict with the uniform line language. |
| 32 Rembrandt-like light | Start from a readable bright image, add a coherent lighting map, lost edges, restrained glaze and bristled impasto in lit areas. | Candle, cloud shadow and dust in the light beam. Excess grain dirties faces; copied rectangular patches reveal unpainted backing. |
| 33 Rubber-hose cartoon | Original rounded silhouettes, flexible limbs, gloves if appropriate, limited monochrome and aged film treatment. | Around 12 drawings/s, beat-led bounce, anticipation and delayed accessories. Film noise inflates apparent motion without improving performance. |
| 34 Chinese shadow puppetry | Translucent colored pierced pieces, joint pivots, rods, cloth screen and warm rear light. | About 10 to 12 drawings/s, wrist dangle and delayed tail joints. Overlapping pieces darken; place those overlaps away from focal faces. |
| 35 Cinematic anime light | Select a specific direction; cel shadows, warm/cool separation, layered cloud masses, reflected light and controlled bloom. | Clouds, curtain and its real shadow, a glass droplet and plant movement. Keep flare inside the light source region; a droplet is not a solid bubble. |
| 36 Picasso blue-period study | Elongated shape, broad low-contrast strokes, decisive non-boiling dark contours and blue tonal hierarchy. | Slow weighted action, sea and reflected light. Use luminance to distinguish identity features; stray orange can destroy the monochrome intent. |
| 37 Flat outlined cartoon | Original semantic SVG, hard-edged fills, consistent round joins; source example used about 2.35 px outlines at 1920-wide. | Separate eyes, lids, head, arms and prop pivots. Deterministic pose control is not a ready-made walking rig. Do not copy named characters. |

## Later research recipes, 38 to 55

These are hypotheses with source prototypes, not mature cross-scene results. They must not inherit older source ratings or performance claims. Several were explicitly below the upstream quality threshold after review.

| ID and range | Distinct language | New-scene test and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 38 Giotto | Weighty simple volume, meaningful gestures, gaze relationships and matte fresco color. | People handing over a heavy object tests contact. Halos and chapel stars are content choices, not mandatory style tokens. Source faces/hands remained too simple. |
| 39 Piero della Francesca | Measured intervals, coherent geometry, clear volume and even light. Preserve medium differences. | An urgent movement through a modern space can retain order. Quiet appearance is not a slow-motion rule. |
| 40 Raphael | Related groups, continuous contours, natural gestures and soft volume, with clear information hierarchy. | A cooperative repair scene tests connections. Do not force every shot into a central triangle or borrow the School of Athens layout. |
| 41 Michelangelo | Structural force, compression, twist, weight and clear foreshortening. | A person opening a jammed door tests force through clothing. Ovals masquerading as anatomy fail; source prototypes lacked true torso twist. |
| 42 Guardi | Broken, dry highlights, silver/warm-gray air, small figures and depth-dependent loose edges. | An industrial hall without water tests atmosphere. Avoid repeated brick-like strokes and missing sparkle. |
| 43 Ambrogio Lorenzetti | Readable civic space, multiple connected activities and continuity across architecture. | A modern workshop or street tests spatial relationships. Sparse identical figures do not make a functioning city. |
| 44 Pietro da Cortona | Baroque group movement, spatial diagonals, dramatic light and strong foreshortening. | A coordinated action tests mass and directional force. Drapes alone do not supply the missing anatomy or depth. |
| 45 Renoir | Warm skin/light relationships, connected people, soft/hard edge transitions and finer face/hand strokes. | A new cooperative scene tests figures and environment together. Keep structure; avoid doll faces and uniform crowds. |
| 46 Pissarro | Directional small touches organize streets, work and inhabited space. Keep later pointillist work a separate range. | A contemporary street tests surface direction and depth. Separate it from Sisley by observed paint organization, not only color. |
| 47 Sisley | Unified environmental light, light broken strokes, simplified distance and legible near structures. | A dry modern setting tests more than sky-and-water motifs. Rebuild large figures rather than enlarging tiny silhouette people. |
| 48 Gauguin | Synthetist flat symbolic color, rounded contour, foreground cropping and deliberate spatial divisions. | New content tests large color relationships. Avoid angular meme-like figures or assuming tropical subject matter is required. |
| 49 Chagall | Poetic space, expressive scale and gravity, with connected emotional color. Distinguish periods and media. | A moving-house memory tests narrative relations. Floating objects alone are not a style. |
| 50 Ma Yuan | Off-center weight, meaningful blank space, forceful directional ink and controlled distant washes. | A winter platform or opened tool tests modern subject transfer. Cluster unequal curved branches; equal vertical lines read as a fence. |
| 51 Persian miniature | Select a period, such as late Timurid Herat or early Safavid Tabriz. Raised viewpoints, opened architecture, layered events and fine pattern. | A multilevel modern library tests expanded space. Patterns attach to surfaces; no fake calligraphy or generic pan-regional ornament. |
| 52 Digital cut-paper cartoon | Original geometric silhouettes, paper hierarchy, small expression changes and limited pivoted performance. | A new adult cast handles a practical mishap. Sliding a whole body is not walking; avoid copied costumes and identical character molds. |
| 53 Feature-animation family | Select a specific film's shape, material, light and acting language. Distinguish Luca from Soul and its separate worlds. | A faceless machine can show weight and causality. Do not claim automatic 3D character modeling, binding or cloth simulation. |
| 55 Northern Dynasties Dunhuang | Earth red or cream bands, mineral blue/green, white animals, narrow jagged mountains, fine red-brown lines and horizontally trailing ribbons. | Select period first. Northern Dynasties and High Tang are different ranges. Source prototype lacked layered mountains and half-turned volume; portrait and narration use were unverified. |

## Material starting points

For a 1920 by 1080 authored scene, use these historical examples as starting values, then inspect the actual result:

- Directional oil painting: region-specific coarse and fine passes. The source's post-impressionist study used cell/length/width of 10/22/6.5 for details, 14/64/8 for walls and 12/46/8 for floor. Do not carry over its early full-screen seed swapping; later evidence showed flicker.
- Blue-period study: broad wall strokes around cell 34, length 110 and width 34, with low contrast; much finer strokes looked like rain or wool.
- Mosaic: use fixed contour-aligned tiles and narrow seams. Letter strokes need roughly two tiles of width, or use a native title above the painting.
- Black-figure: near-black #1B120D, terracotta #DC6A2F, incised #E8904C and restrained #8A2A37/#F6EAD6 additions. Incision, silhouette and added color matter more than random texture.
- Light study: paint bright shapes first, then apply a coherent light map. Keep a little separation light behind the shadow side. Bristle highlights need uneven fibers, not three perfectly parallel stripes.
- Ink: preserve paper, variable brush width, dry-brush gaps and controlled wash spread. Do not fill deliberate negative space with particles to increase apparent motion.

## Research acceptance

Write five signature traits as required, useful or conflicting. Compare output against actual references, including a nearby art language. Look for credible medium at full scale and physical relations in silhouettes. A color palette plus noise is not sufficient.

The source weighted signature fidelity 35, medium 20, composition/story 15, shape 15, motion 10 and technical behavior 5. These are review prompts, not objective quality measurements. Missing core signatures or crude subjects can invalidate a high average. Record observable facts, separate creator taste from self-score, and leave a recipe in research status when evidence is weak. Inspect motion independently from still-frame appeal.

For further study, [source reference links](source-reference-links.md) preserve selected upstream primary sources. Those links have not been visually reviewed during this conversion. Read only the relevant section when researching a chosen direction.
