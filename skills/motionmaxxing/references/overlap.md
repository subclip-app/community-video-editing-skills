# Overlap with other UI and SaaS skills

| Topic | Already covered by | What motionmaxxing adds |
|---|---|---|
| Layer contract, validation, `add_runtime_layer` | `hyperframes-motion-layers` | Not repeated; `runtime.md` only covers the Motion runtime inside a layer. |
| Launch-film scene recipes, one shape that never cuts, springs | `product-launch-motion` | Not a recipe book. It decides what to film first: an idea only this brand could own, a built world, a beat table where every beat buys one belief. |
| Promo shot recipes and beat cuts | `video-shotcraft` | Shot-to-shot handoffs (the last frame of A is the first frame of B) and cuts on motion, with numbers from measured moments. |
| Launch films as code, house style | `huashu-motion` (being added) | Rules against a house style: the look comes from the subject's brand, never from a reference or a previous film. |
| UI taste and critique vocabulary | `taste-skill`, `impeccable` (being added) | Taste applied to time: 12 eases and durations measured frame by frame on 53 professional moments, and six review gates checked on the frames, with the times written down instead of self-certified. |
| Easing and timing craft | `motion-polish`, `code-video-recipes` | Measured curves with their share of real moves and what professionals used each for (`measured-curves.md`). |

In short: measured curves from professional work, a brand-owned idea, and honest review gates.
