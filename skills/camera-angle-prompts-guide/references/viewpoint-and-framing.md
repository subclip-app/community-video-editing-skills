## 14. POV

**Feeling:** First person: the viewer becomes a character in the scene.

**Camera sentence:** A first-person hand-held view, as if the viewer is walking up to [subject] in [place] with a gentle natural step; no hands and no other person visible; [subject] turns to the viewer and smiles.

**Tip:** Say no hands are visible unless you want them, or the model adds a stranger's hands.

**In Subclip:** Generate it. For a quick fake, use a gently shaking crop of a forward walking clip.

## 24. Insert

**Feeling:** A detail shot that tells the story: hands, objects, the small stuff.

**Camera sentence:** A tight detail shot of the hands of [subject] turning [an object] slowly on [a surface], [light] curling through [steam, dust or smoke]; the face stays out of frame.

**Tip:** Say the face stays out of frame, or the model tilts up to find it.

**In Subclip:** Native: crop the hands from a medium clip with sourceZoom. Generate if the object is not in any source.

## 29. Hidden camera

**Feeling:** We feel watched: part of the frame is blocked by a real object.

**Camera sentence:** The camera peeks from low behind [the edge of a shelf or wall] that blocks part of the frame in dark wood and clutter, watching [subject] alone near [window]. They sense it and look straight at the lens with a slow knowing smile.

**Tip:** Block part of the frame with a named real object so the camera reads as hidden.

**In Subclip:** Native: a dark overlay shape on one side plus vignette and a slow drift. See ai-camera-shots for the security-cam look.

## 31. Snorricam

**Feeling:** The face stays locked while the world swings around it.

**Camera sentence:** The camera is rigged to the chest of [subject] and faces back at them; their face stays steady in the centre of the frame while they walk across [place] and the room bobs and sways behind them.

**Tip:** Say the camera is rigged to the chest facing back, so the face stays centred as they walk.

**In Subclip:** Generate it. A native fake would need a masked face layer over a shaking background, which is fragile.
