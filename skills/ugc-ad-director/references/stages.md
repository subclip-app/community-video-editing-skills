# Stage gates

How the gate works, and what to do when it is tempting to cheat.

## The gate

Each stage has one required output and one confirmation. Before every reply, check silently: which stage am I in, was its output delivered, did the creator confirm it? If the answer to either is no, deliver the missing output or wait. Do not move on.

Closing a stage: "Stage N complete: <output>." Then one question and stop. Never stack a second question.

## Flow in order

1. Ask: new character (A) or upload (B). Stop.
2. A: show the character form (see `sheets.md`), build the base portrait, ask "Is this look OK, or do you want changes?" Stop.
   B: take up to 10 photos, write the identity analysis, ask "Is my analysis correct?" Stop.
3. Build the 8-panel character sheet. Show it. Give the sheet prompt, a negative prompt and a short character summary. Ask "Approve this character sheet?" Stop. On yes, say the identity is locked.
4. Ask if the creator has a product photo (yes, or later). Stop. Analyze it, write product notes, build the product sheet, ask for approval. Lock.
5. Ask if the creator has a location photo or wants one made. Stop. Build the location notes and sheet, ask for approval. Lock.
6. Write 10 concepts. Ask which one. Stop.
7. Ask for duration. Stop.
8. Write the script. Ask for approval. Stop.
9. Write the storyboard plan table. Ask for approval. Stop.
10. Build the storyboard image. Ask for approval. Stop.
11. Run the consistency check, show the report, ask whether to move on to the clip plan. Stop.
12. Quote credits, then build.

## Rules for edits and re-dos

- A change request inside a stage re-runs that stage only. Say what will change and what stays.
- A change in an earlier locked asset (a new product colour, another kitchen) reopens that stage and every stage after it. Tell the creator the cost before agreeing.
- No product photo: do not invent a real-looking product silently. Offer to continue with a placeholder and mark the product sheet as pending, or pause. A fictional product for a demo is fine if the creator says so.
- Outfit and hair changes are allowed only on request and must keep the face and body locked.

## Things that fail the gate

- Showing a single portrait and calling stage 1 done.
- Asking to "approve the character" before the sheet exists.
- Putting storyboard panels inside the location sheet, or a location inside the storyboard.
- Writing the clip plan before the storyboard image is approved.
- Asking two questions in one reply.
