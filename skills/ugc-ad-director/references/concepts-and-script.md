# Concepts, duration and script

## Ten concepts (text only)

Only after the three sheets are locked. Write exactly 10 original concepts for a modern audience on short-video platforms. No storyboard images and no shot lists yet. Mix formats, and match the product category (do not make every idea about technology).

Formats to draw from: selfie review, first reaction, problem and fix, "stop scrolling" warning, routine or day-in-the-life, comparison with the old way, myth versus fact, unboxing, "things I wish I knew", POV, over-the-shoulder demo, honest two-week update.

Each concept has: a title, the format, the hook line (first 1 to 3 seconds), the problem or curiosity, how the product enters, the demo moment, the camera style (selfie, propped, POV, over-shoulder), the on-screen text idea and the soft call to action. Ask "Which one do you want to make?" and stop. Lock the pick.

## Duration

Ask once: 15, 20, 30 or 45 seconds. Longer asks for more clips and more credits; say so.

## Script

Fit the chosen length. The shape is: hook (0 to 3 s), relatable problem or curiosity, product enters naturally, demo or experience, benefit in everyday terms, soft call to action.

Spoken lines read like a friend talking: contractions, short sentences, one idea each, a believable opening ("ok so I have to show you this") and no ad copy ("revolutionary", "game-changing"). Soft endings only ("link's below if you want it", "worth a look"); never pressure.

Claims: describe what is shown, not guaranteed results. No health, income or medical claims, no invented stats, no fake discount or scarcity unless the creator supplied it. A comparison must be true and about the creator's own product only.

Give a table: time range, spoken line, on-screen text (bold keyword captions in the platform style), action. Count about 2.5 words per second for the spoken total and flag it if the script is too long for the length. Offer an AI disclosure line for the post caption when the character or voice is synthetic. Ask for approval and stop.
