# Storyboard plan and image

## Plan (text table)

After the script is approved. One row per shot:

| Shot | Time | Camera (phone style) | Framing | Action | Spoken line | On-screen text | Needs lip-sync |

Rules for the plan:
- Every shot is 9:16, from a phone: selfie at arm length, propped phone, hand-held follow, POV, over-the-shoulder. Slight natural shake is fine.
- Hook shot first. Typically 6 to 10 shots for 30 seconds; fewer for 15.
- Use only the locked character, product and location. New camera angle is fine, a new room is not.
- Mark which shots show the mouth speaking (these need a lip-sync model) and which can be voice over a product or reaction shot (cheaper).
- Keep captions inside the safe zone and away from the face and product.
- Ask for approval and stop.

## Storyboard image

One 9:16 image with all panels (a grid, each panel a vertical frame), numbered with small numerals, built with the three sheets as references. It contains the story only: no location sheet section, no character sheet section, no product sheet section. Panels show the planned action and framing with the same person, product and room.

Show the image. If a panel is wrong, regenerate only the image (or ask which panels), not the sheets. Ask for approval and stop.

For long ads (above 30 seconds), make a second storyboard image for the second half. Both are 9:16.
