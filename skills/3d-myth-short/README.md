# 3D myth short

<!-- cli:start -->
> Install from your terminal: `npx @subclip/community-skills install 3d-myth-short`
<!-- cli:end -->

Turns one idea into a narrated 30 second 3D animated short. It states the story everyone believes, flips it, shows the real mechanism and ends on a kicker. Characters, places and objects are locked once as reference sheets so every shot matches.

## Use it

Tell your agent: "Use 3d-myth-short to make a short about <idea>". It writes the brief for your approval, makes the reference sheets, generates three 10 second blocks, voices the narration and cuts it on the Subclip timeline.

## What changed for Subclip

This version keeps the original workflow for scripting and visual continuity. Generate video with `generate_ai_media`, make stills in Codex or Subclip, and use Subclip speech tools for the narrator. The finished timeline keeps the visuals, narration and captions editable.

## Finished reference

A workshop globe and two patches of light show why the seasons change. The January reveal sets up the final snowman gag. [Watch the narrated reference](examples/seasons-globe.mp4). [Watch the silent hero](preview.mp4).
