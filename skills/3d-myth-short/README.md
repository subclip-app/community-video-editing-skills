# 3D myth short

Turns one idea into a narrated 30 second 3D animated short. It states the story everyone believes, flips it, shows the real mechanism and ends on a kicker. Characters, places and objects are locked once as reference sheets so every shot matches.

## Use it

Tell your agent: "Use 3d-myth-short to make a short about <idea>". It writes the brief for your approval, makes the reference sheets, generates three 10 second blocks, voices the narration and cuts it on the Subclip timeline.

## What changed for Subclip

The original skill runs in Claude Code and hands you prompts to paste into outside generators, then cuts with ffmpeg. This version keeps the creative method and does the work in Subclip: generation through `generate_ai_media` (or your own Codex CLI for images), voice through Subclip speech tools, and assembly on the editable timeline. Nothing here needs an outside key, ffmpeg or a script.
