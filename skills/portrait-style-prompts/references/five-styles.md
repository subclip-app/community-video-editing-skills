# Five style slot sheets

Fill slot 1 from the real frame (subject, pose, clothes, setting). The sheets below give slots 2 to 7. The test scene for these looks was a man in a dark suit in an office cubicle with a diagonal beam of light; swap in your own scene.

## 1 Anime

- Medium: cel-shaded 2D anime, bold clean linework, flat saturated colours with sharp graphic shadow blocks.
- Light: a hard directional beam cutting diagonally, sharp highlight edges on hair and clothes.
- Wardrobe: crisp, exaggerated fit with sharp collar lines.
- Era: modern with a late-90s Japanese animation edge.
- Grade: high saturation, warm skin against cool blue-grey surroundings.
- Mood: intense, dramatic, tense.

## 2 Watercolour

- Medium: soft pigment washes with visible bleed and paper grain; loose broken edges instead of clean lines.
- Light: a diffused beam, soft glow, no hard edges.
- Wardrobe: muted tones blending at the edges; texture implied, not defined.
- Era: timeless, faintly vintage.
- Grade: desaturated pastels, warm ochres and greys, occasional ink-black accents.
- Mood: reflective, gentle, painterly calm.

## 3 Pixel art

- Medium: 16-bit retro game rendering, blocky low-resolution forms, a visible pixel grid, 16 to 32 colours.
- Light: the beam as a flat dithered gradient stripe.
- Wardrobe: simplified geometric shapes, iconic silhouette over detail.
- Era: early-90s console game.
- Grade: CRT-style palette, slightly oversaturated primaries.
- Mood: nostalgic, playful, energetic.

## 4 Noir comic

- Medium: high-contrast black and white ink illustration, heavy inked shadows, halftone dot shading, bold panel linework.
- Light: hard chiaroscuro slicing diagonally, deep black pools on the unlit side of the face.
- Wardrobe: sharp pinstripes in stark contrast, no midtones.
- Era: 1940s hardboiled detective in a modern setting.
- Grade: pure black and white, no colour.
- Mood: tense, moody, cinematic menace.

## 5 Paper cut

- Medium: layered cut-paper collage; hair, face, clothes and background are separate paper layers with visible edge shadows, fine paper grain and slight drop shadows.
- Light: soft raking light from the upper left; the beam as a cut-paper light shape with soft shadows beneath each edge.
- Wardrobe: flat solid-colour paper shapes with clean die-cut edges; folds implied by layer offsets.
- Era: modern editorial collage.
- Grade: matte muted tones, warm cream and charcoal, one warm accent for skin against cool blue-grey paper.
- Mood: crafted, tactile, quietly stylised.

## Writing your own style

Pick a real medium (not an adjective), say what the light does inside that medium, say how cloth is drawn, add a time reference, set the palette and saturation, and end with a mood. Keep each slot to one or two phrases. If the image comes back too photographic, strengthen slot 2 and add "redraw everything in the medium, nothing photographic".
