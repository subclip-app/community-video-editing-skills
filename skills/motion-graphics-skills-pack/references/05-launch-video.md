# 05 Launch video (covered by another skill)

**Status:** covered. Use `product-launch-motion` for the full workflow, and `video-shotcraft` for real-capture product shots, or `website-to-video` when the source is a URL. This pack adds nothing to them except two small habits.

The upstream skill builds a 30 to 45 second launch video: one sentence split across three to five beats, real product screens with slow push-ins, a typed prompt inside a real-looking input box, a proof beat, a number counted up with its source, and a call to action word for word as on the sales page. Never invent a price, date, spots-left count, testimonial or result.

## Two habits worth keeping

- **Seven-beat sheet** (45 s): hook words 0 to 4 s, proof 4 to 9, product working 9 to 17, a quality beat (a check that passes) 17 to 22, how it works 22 to 32, the number that matters 32 to 38, name and call to action 38 to 45 (hold at least 3 s). Show the sheet and wait for a yes.
- **Build the first 10 seconds in two or three styles first**, pick one, then build the rest.
