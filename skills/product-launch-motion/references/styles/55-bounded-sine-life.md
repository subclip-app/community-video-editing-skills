# 55 Bounded sine settle and pulse

Group: Motion grammar
Use it for: Keep one hero element alive during a hold.

## Recipe (in our own words, swap the [brackets])

- 2 px settle on a numeral, one 9 percent pulse on pills. Envelope any idle to zero 0.3 s before the end. At most one element alive in a hold.
