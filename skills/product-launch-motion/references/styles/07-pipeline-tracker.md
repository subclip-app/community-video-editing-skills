# 07 Pipeline tracker

Group: Scene types (shots)
Use it for: Fulfilment, orders, builds, jobs, approvals: steps completing without you.

## Recipe (in our own words, swap the [brackets])

- Split frame: tracker card on one side, evidence grid on the other. The card shows a real subject (item, recipient), never bare states.
- Three states complete 0.18 s apart: circle pops 0.6 to 1, ring ping, connector bar draws (scaleY from the top).
- The last state completes as the first evidence card lands. Cards rise, one sheen sweeps each, pill appears 0.14 s later.
- Counter chip counts on the payoff word, every pill pulses once from one shared sin driver.
