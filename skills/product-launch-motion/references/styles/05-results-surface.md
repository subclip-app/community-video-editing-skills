# 05 Results surface

Group: Scene types (shots)
Use it for: The product answers and the answer is specific: lists, grids, tables, feeds.

## Recipe (in our own words, swap the [brackets])

- One hero card with a static 3D tilt, drawn natively: query bar, chip row, result count, [3 to 5] rows.
- Each row has a skeleton overlay using the same geometry; skeletons resolve one at a time, 0.2 s apart.
- Enter with a lateral blur streak (blur on a wrapper, not on the 3D element). One scan line sweeps once.
- Only the control the cursor clicks gets a pop. A slow turntable (7 degrees over 4 s) and a push to 1.06 on the detail beat.

## Prompt to give the layer builder

Build a [10 s] [1920x1080] layer for [fictional or real product, brand colours]. Style: Results surface. Follow the recipe above. Copy and numbers: [your approved copy and figures].
