# 02 Crowding problem

Group: Scene types (shots)
Use it for: Agitate a workflow problem: tool sprawl, handoffs, manual steps.

## Recipe (in our own words, swap the [brackets])

- [6 to 9] labelled chips in three depth planes; near is biggest and sharpest, far is smallest and softest.
- Start positions come from the index (golden angle), never random. Chips land one per beat, 0.14 s apart, with a tally 1 to n in the margin.
- On the turning conjunction every chip moves inward, the far plane racks sharp, vignette rises.
- Payoff line [one tool takes all of them] appears in the opened centre; crowd dims to 0.3; a hand-drawn underline draws last.
- Measure the underline with offsetLeft and offsetWidth, not getBoundingClientRect.
