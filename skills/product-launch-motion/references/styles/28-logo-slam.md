# 28 Logo slam

Group: Flourishes (v2)
Use it for: Customer logos one per beat instead of a trusted-by row. Only brands that really ran with you.

## Recipe (in our own words, swap the [brackets])

- One shape resized and recoloured on every hit. Beats accelerate: quarter notes, eighths, sixteenths. Never recolour real logos you do not own the right to show.
