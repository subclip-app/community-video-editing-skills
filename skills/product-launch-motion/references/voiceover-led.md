# When the film is narrated (voice-led variant)

The upstream keeps its first version for voiceover-led explainers: [legacy/SKILL.md](https://github.com/AbubakrChan/product-launch-motion/blob/main/legacy/SKILL.md) and its twelve references. Its ten laws, adapted:

1. Truth first: write the approved-figures list before animating; refuse any other number.
2. Word-locked sync: cue every reveal to the measured start of the word it illustrates. Make the voice first (`generate_local_speech`, a voice that matches the speaker), import it, take word times from the dynamic captions or a transcript, then build.
3. Determinism: no random, no timers, no CSS transitions. Derive scatter from the index.
4. One camera, two nodes: dolly on the outer node, turn on the inner node (style 57).
5. The cursor is a stage prop: about 44 x 54 px at 1920, white fill, dark stroke, shadow, inside the camera node (styles 33 and 61).
6. Sound is arithmetic: level the asset, do not rely on volume. Verify the cue in the exported file.
7. Nothing ends moving: resolve every shot before the cut (styles 51 and 56).
8. Ration the accent: two grounds, one accent, a written budget for where it may appear (style 62).
9. Green gates are not sufficient: look at frames from the delivered file.
10. No house style: three directions, kill two, name a signature move (style 68).

Pacing: a shot ends a breath after its last word. Hold hero frames longer than the voice. Expect to lengthen holds by about 10 percent after the first watch.
