# Real visuals and the AI-tells ban list

Adapted from the style-edit skill and AI tells list of nextwork-projects/ai-video-editor-cooked (MIT). An edit that looks like every other AI edit is a failed edit.

## What goes on screen, in this order

1. **The creator's own assets.** Their photos, screenshots, b-roll and clips. A clip of their own is a video item on the line.
2. **The real thing, captured.** A named product, website, doc or article: the real page cropped to the evidence, with the line marked. A quoted post: a capture of the post. An app: its real UI. A video or repo: its real page. Capture with Subclip's recorder or have the creator supply the file.
3. **A stat shown inside its real source.** The page that published the number, with the number ringed or highlighted as it is said. Never a big number on a plain ground.
4. **UI recreated from real parts**, when the speaker describes what an app does: real names and logos, messages in the speaker's words, real commands, the app's real notifications.
5. **Real logos** for brands named in passing; a cluster of logos around the head when several are named together; a flow diagram of real logos for how work moves between named things.
6. **No type cards.** Words on a plain ground (a slam, a title, a counter, a chart, a checklist, a lower third) are replaced by the real source, captured and marked. At most about four words of text on any card, as a label. On-screen words come from the speaker or the source. No invented stats, no invented copy.

Pacing: when a style is copied, its card rate wins. Never two cards at once. Never cover the speaker's face (a card may tuck behind the head only when a cutout is on). A line with nothing real to show stays empty and shows the speaker. Never ask what to show: pick the real capture.

Vertical frames are filled by the real product or the speaker. Vertical captures need a crop to the evidence so it reads on a phone. Nothing important leaves the safe frame: focus, words and cursor stay inside on every frame.

Privacy in captures: use a clean browser profile, personal data blurred (including the logged-in account's own name and handle), and never press anything that creates, deletes, pays, publishes or invites unless the creator said yes for that video.

## Ban list

BAN means never unless the creator's own brand or request names it. WARN means allowed with a stated reason.

Color
- BAN: warm cream or beige paper grounds; one flat saturated ground (vermilion, forest green, cobalt) filling a card; near-black panel lit by one acid accent (neon on black); purple or indigo accents and purple-to-blue gradients.
- WARN: smooth two-color gradient grounds; orange-teal or sepia casts; a palette nobody chose. Color comes from the real content, the creator's brand or the measured style.

Type
- BAN: default grotesk display faces (Inter, Geist, Archivo, Roboto, Poppins, Montserrat, Manrope, DM Sans) for headings. Use a serif or the style's measured display face.
- WARN: one accented word in a headline; small tracked-out eyebrow labels; Title Case On Every Word; middle-dot meta strings; 01 / 02 / 03 on things that are not steps.

Layout
- BAN: the title card (big centered heading, small grey line under it, short accent bar).
- WARN: side-tab accent stripes; bento grids of mismatched tiles; floating pills and badges. One exhibit at a time.

Icons and decoration
- BAN: emoji anywhere; sparkle, wand, rocket, star or bot icons for "AI" or "launch"; cards whose only pictures are stock line icons; frosted glass panels; glow or neon halos. Crisp edges, depth from a real shadow.
- WARN: isometric or 3D blob filler.

Motion
- WARN: every card entering the same way (entrance by what the card is: a post slides, a capture lifts, a logo pops); bounce or elastic on everything (expo or quart out, one overshoot on one hero moment); idle bobbing while held (land, then hold still); too-smooth camera with no jitter (cut instead of glide); Ken Burns on every still; the same designed transition on every scene (hard cuts for most); a punch zoom on every sentence.

Captions
- WARN: uppercase heavy-stroke yellow-active-word one-word-at-a-time presets unless the style measures them; keyword color roulette. Use the style's measured caption look, one highlight color or none.
- BAN: auto emoji beside keywords.

Copy
- BAN: slop words (unlock, supercharge, game-changer, level up, seamless, elevate, revolutionize, effortless, harness, empower, leverage, delve, unleash, skyrocket, cutting-edge); "200x faster" multiplier stat cards.
- WARN: "It's not just X, it's Y", "here's the thing", "the future of", "changes everything"; stat counters standing alone; labels that repeat what the picture says; em dashes and padded triples in on-screen copy.

Imagery
- BAN: AI-generated images as B-roll (plastic skin, gibberish signs, extra fingers, warping backgrounds, flat even light); gibberish UI mockups with fake numbers; text-only cards.
- WARN: generic stock (handshake, laptop typing, city timelapse); a literal stock clip for every noun said. Show the named thing or stay on the face.

Sound
- WARN: a whoosh on every card; the same sound file on most cues; meme sounds (vine boom, airhorn, ka-ching, record scratch) unless the style uses them; an upbeat library bed at constant level under everything.
- BAN: a flat TTS voice with no breaths when the creator's own voice exists. Keep the speaker's real voice and breaths.

Thumbnails and social graphics
- WARN: the shocked-face-plus-red-arrow formula; the same Canva-style template on every post. Use the style's measured layout.

## Final check before showing the creator

Walk every placed card and caption against this list. Fix what fails, then re-check. Report any WARN you kept and the reason.
