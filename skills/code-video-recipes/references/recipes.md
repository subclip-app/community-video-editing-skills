# Subclip recipes

Use the example cues as starting timings. Change them to fit the subject, source media and requested duration. Creator links lead to the original posts. The [catalog](catalog.md) links each original prompt record.

## Motion graphics

### Widget entrance and line draw

**Brief.** Scale a card from about one-third size while fading it in. Settle the main silhouette before revealing the heading, values and badges in reading order. Draw the graph stroke from left to right. Bring up the back glow under the contents and sweep two soft highlights a little apart.

**Build.** Use one transparent HyperFrames layer with an SVG stroke reveal and staggered GSAP transitions. Keep the graph endpoint, card padding and title clear of the sweeps. Put replaceable labels and colors in variables. Try 0 to 0.5 seconds for entry, 0.5 to 1.5 for the reveal, and a short settled hold. Check that the headline stays legible and the last point appears before the hold. [@Khlaseek_dsgner](https://x.com/Khlaseek_dsgner/status/2108167809997877659)

### Silence into a music machine

**Brief.** Fill a warm paper field with bold geometric letters. Let the letters become ramps, gears, keys and a drum in black, cobalt and yellow. Save the first sound for the ball drop. Every strike has a visible contact. Finish on one decisive title hit and a quiet hold.

**Build.** Try a 20-second structure with silent assembly from 0 to 12, percussion from 12 to 16 and an end card through 20. Place approved percussion in native Audio items. Mark the sound starts, then drive ball, gear and key poses from those times. Match the final frame of assembly to the opening pose of the performance layer. Place the final title in its own item if it does not share the shape transformation. Check a frame just before every hit, at visible contact and just afterward. Listen for early ticks during the silent setup. [@adrianduta96](https://x.com/adrianduta96/status/2109187815195992248)

### Deterministic butterfly loop

**Brief.** Make the wing shape, pose and contrast readable in one glance. Repeat a wing cycle with a shared phase that can also drive small counter or diagram changes.

**Build.** Use one 12-second HyperFrames scene if that duration fits. Draw the wings as mirrored paths and compute the pose from normalized, repeating local time. Keep random texture or particles seeded. Match the mathematical pose at the boundary to the first frame and inspect the last visible frame for a visible hitch. Revisit a middle timestamp after seeking out of order. [@0xKarmi](https://x.com/0xKarmi/status/2108495927988129839)

### Product workflow launch

**Brief.** Make a short film around one real task in the creator's product. Open with the outcome, show the minimum cursor and typing actions that explain it, then return to a readable result.

**Build.** Use a 15 to 30 second target if appropriate. Trim real captures in native media items. Reframe with eased media keyframes and hold each result long enough to read it. Add a separate cursor or callout layer only when the capture needs it. Use a native 3D device layer for a supported hero shot, with the capture bound to its screen. For a creator-requested Remotion treatment, use the internal Remotion skill. Check the UI at normal playback size during the zoom and settle. [@Screeendev](https://x.com/Screeendev/status/2108333630623981790)

## Explainers

### Measured ingredient build

**Brief.** Start with an empty vessel. Add one ingredient at a time, with its name and quantity visible during the pour. Let fill level, color and garnish show real progress. End on the completed result.

**Build.** Use a short HyperFrames scene for a stylized version. Clip moving liquid and highlights to the glass shape. Keep the ingredient labels in separate text items and verify the recipe quantities with the creator. For a narrated 30-second version, import the voice first and let each phrase anchor a pour and a readable hold. Check each fill boundary for jumps and watch for text crossing the glass rim. [@Ror_Fly](https://x.com/Ror_Fly/status/2102853258582880547)

### Force and failure cutaway

**Brief.** Introduce a familiar object, show force traveling through it, then replay the region of interest with a consistent color scale and a close-up. The progression should read before the voice explains it.

**Build.** Use a custom HyperFrames scene for a mechanism made from original geometry, or a vector section view. Use restrained camera changes. Stage one visible change per cycle and keep a persistent legend. Use creator-supplied validated stress values when making quantitative claims. Label illustrated fields as schematic. Add narration and captions natively. Inspect the load moment, a middle cycle and the final close-up. [@chang_](https://x.com/chang_/status/2108458503367200908)

## 3D scenes

### Exploded stack

**Brief.** Show the assembled silhouette before moving parts apart along one shared axis. Reveal hidden components and explain them one at a time, then reassemble.

**Build.** For original custom geometry, use the supported HyperFrames Three.js adapter. Define assembled and separated positions for each part and derive the pose from local time. Plan a brief opening orbit, a controlled separation and a readable inspection hold before reassembly. Keep text on native layers when it can remain fixed, or place labels in the scene when they need to track parts. Check for intersections, hidden labels and perspective changes at the half-open pose. [@aichrislee](https://x.com/aichrislee/status/2108347412636938609)

### Material study in a glass lamp

**Brief.** Use a brushed base, a transparent vessel and warm backlighting. Show the interior forms rise, lengthen, divide and drop at distinct points in the cycle.

**Build.** Use original procedural geometry and authored curves inside a deterministic HyperFrames Three.js scene. Change the material or lighting on a timed beat if the brief calls for a temperature or color change. If the scene cannot render acceptably, propose a stylized 2D silhouette with overlapping shapes and a coordinated split. Verify the transition frames and check transparency against the actual project background. [@juancarlos17626](https://x.com/juancarlos17626/status/2108301453907382690)

## Interactive references as linear demos

### Raked garden

**Brief.** Establish the untouched sand. Trace one readable rake stroke past the rocks, reveal parallel grooves and lower the light so the relief becomes clear.

**Build.** Replace dragging with one selected path and a paced reveal. Use SVG stroke offsets and short shadows for a graphic treatment. Use seek-driven geometry and lighting for a dimensional scene. Hold before and after the lighting change. Inspect the boundaries around rocks and keep groove widths legible after scaling. [@arixxbt](https://x.com/arixxbt/status/2108541814718292438)

### Crayon performance

**Brief.** Use uneven outlines, grain and a limited night palette. Let a small character's gestures follow individual notes and use the wider composition to show the shift from a quiet opening to a fuller middle and a soft ending.

**Build.** Align a HyperFrames character and instrument to a cue sheet derived from the approved native piano track. At each cue, name the key, contact frame and release. Move lighting and camera at phrase boundaries. Preserve the grain pattern while seeking unless a deliberate redraw is part of the look. Inspect finger contact and listen for drift early, midway and near the end. [@0xCodez](https://x.com/0xCodez/status/2104257855142474136)
