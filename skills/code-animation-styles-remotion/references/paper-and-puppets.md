# Paper and puppets

Every piece is its own group with a pivot and a drop shadow. Move pieces rigidly; never bend them. Shadows must offset the same way in every layer.

Each recipe below is a short build brief. The example clip named in the title is the visual target: open it before building. The chai film (kettle pours into a clay cup, steam rises, 5 s) is the demo story; replace it with the creator's story using `our-guide.md`.

## 14 Cut-out collage

- Example: `examples/14-cut-out.mp4`
- Look: Paper shapes with torn edges and drop shadows, moved like a cut-out puppet.
- Palette: Craft paper tones, 3 to 4 paper colours, shadow rgba(0,0,0,.25)
- Motion rules: Pieces move rigidly on pivots, shadows offset by layer, step on twos for stop-motion feel.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Storytelling, kids, craft brands
- Also see: paper-cut-motion

## 15 Red papercut

- Example: `examples/15-red-papercut.mp4`
- Look: Red cut paper with lacy patterns and flowers on a warm background.
- Palette: Red #c8202a on warm cream #f6e7d0, soft inner shadow
- Motion rules: Symmetric lacework appears in pieces, flowers unfold, everything one colour of paper.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Celebrations, cultural stories, greetings
- Also see: paper-cut-motion; ai-style-atlas 24

## 16 Paper pop-up

- Example: `examples/16-paper-pop-up.mp4`
- Look: A pop-up book page: layered card that stands up with folds and shadows.
- Palette: Card tones, visible fold lines, depth shadows per layer
- Motion rules: Layers rise from flat to upright with an eased rotateX, shadows lengthen as they rise.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG plus CSS 3D)
- Best for: Story intros, children, product reveals
- Also see: paper-cut-motion

## 17 Shadow puppet

- Example: `examples/17-shadow-puppet.mp4`
- Look: Black puppets with cut-out holes on rods, lit from behind on a glowing screen.
- Palette: Screen glow #ffd9a0 to #ff9a4a, puppets pure black
- Motion rules: Rigid puppets on visible rods, small sway, light flicker is slow and subtle.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Myths, folk tales, calm narration
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 03
