# Our guide: choosing and retelling a style

## 1. Choose a style by topic and audience
| Topic | Good styles | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How something works, engineering | 4 Blueprint, 26 Math diagram, 37 Circuit board, 36 Hologram HUD | Lines and labels carry the explanation |
| Teaching, lessons, tutorials | 1 Whiteboard, 2 Chalkboard, 3 Notepad doodle | Drawing on screen matches the pace of explaining |
| Software, AI, data | 36 Hologram HUD, 37 Circuit board, 11 Flat vector, 38 Liquid motion, 39 Generative abstract | Clean, modern, tech tone |
| Finance, premium, heritage | 22 Art deco, 19 Engraving, 29 Glass product | Calm, expensive feel |
| Food, drink, wellness, nature | 8 Watercolour, 6 Urban sketch, 21 Ukiyo-e, 29 Glass product | Soft and warm |
| Kids and families | 7 Crayon book, 30 Claymation, 28 Brick toy, 23 Cut-out | Tactile and friendly |
| Games and retro | 31 Pixel art RPG, 27 Low-poly island, 24 Memphis diorama | Familiar to the audience |
| History, folk tales, culture | 18 Woodcut, 17 Shadow puppet, 15 Red papercut, 23 Stained glass, 32 Silent film, 33 Newsreel | Period feel |
| Music, youth, launches | 12 80s cel anime, 20 Silkscreen poster, 25 Sticker explainer | Loud, graphic, short |
| Logos, intros, transitions | 9 One line, 38 Liquid motion, 10 Cel line boil | Short, abstract, brandable |
| Science and nature close-ups | 35 Microscope, 34 Lidar survey | The look says "science" |

Audience checks: kids need big shapes and bright colour; professionals need quiet palettes and little text; vertical social needs one idea per clip and bold shapes; older or formal audiences suit print and engraving looks. Offer three candidates with their example clips; never show more than three at once.

## 2. Retell the story with a new one
The collection fixes one story and lets every style redraw it. Do the same: write the story first, then draw it in the chosen style.

The six beats of the 5 second demo (frames at 30 fps):
| Frames | Beat | In the chai film |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 24 | Actor enters | The kettle slides in from the right |
| 24 to 42 | Gets ready | It tilts |
| 42 to 96 | Main action | Chai pours in a stream |
| 44 to 100 | Result grows | The cup fills |
| 96 to 126 | Finish | Kettle tilts back and leaves |
| 104 to 150 | Aftermath | Steam rises |

Write your own table before drawing:
| Idea | Actor | Action | Result | Aftermath |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee into a mug | Coffee pot | Coffee stream | Mug fills | Steam |
| Watering a plant | Can | Water drops | Soil darkens, sprout grows | Two leaves open |
| Saving money | Hand with coin | Coin drops | Piggy bank fills | Sparkles |
| Data upload | Cloud | Packets travel a path | Gauge fills | Tick appears |
| New feature launch | Phone | Light beam | Card fills with the feature | Badge pops |

Rules:
1. Keep the beats and timing the same in every style of one story. Change only the look. Styles then compare cleanly and a restyle ("now in pixel art") is a new layer from the same table and shapes.
2. Put the shapes (actor, container, what flows, what rises) in one shapes definition first. Each style version reuses it.
3. Keep the style's own materials, palette logic, filters and motion character (stepped for hand-made, smooth for digital, flicker for film).
4. Never overwrite an earlier style version; add the new layer beside it.
5. A longer story: add beats to the table, give each a start and end time, and build in 5 to 10 second layers.
6. The 3D styles (27, 28, 29, 30, 34) take more work when objects change: describe objects as simple lathe, box or sphere meshes.

## 3. Durations
- Hook or card: 3 to 5 s. The demo film: 5 s.
- Explainer segment: 6 to 10 s per layer, chained.
- Whole piece in one style: up to 60 s. Keep one style unless the creator asks for a mix.
- With narration, build to the voice timing: set each beat's start from the word timings, then hold the aftermath until the sentence ends.
- Loops (preview, backdrops): make the last frame match the first.

## 4. Vertical and safe zones
- The demo films are 4:5 (1080 by 1350). On the Subclip timeline use the project's ratio: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts and Reels, 4:5 for feeds.
- For 9:16 keep the key action in the centre 80 percent. Leave the top 12 percent and the bottom 20 percent free of text and faces, and the right 12 percent free of text (platform buttons).
- Re-lay the shapes for the ratio instead of cropping; a style that is a full-frame board (01, 02, 06) usually only needs the board resized.
- Text at least 56 px high at 1080 wide; thin lines at least 4 px.

## 5. QA checklist
- Story reads: at the main action something is clearly happening, at the result the change is visible, at the end the aftermath shows.
- The style is named from a still frame. Two styles never look like a recolour of each other.
- Nothing clipped at the edges. Actor fully enters and leaves.
- Layering: liquid stays inside the container, stream goes into it, steam starts at the surface.
- Contrast: the main subject stands out from the background.
- Motion character: stepped where hand-made, smooth where digital; no unintended flicker.
- Text: only intended words, spelled right, inside the safe area.
- Content rules in `SKILL.md` pass.

## 6. Cost
Code-drawn layers use no AI credits. Credits come only from optional extras: cloud speech, generated music or generated imagery. Quote and ask first for each.
