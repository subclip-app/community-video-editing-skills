# Graphic and explainer

These carry meaning with shapes and words. Text is short and about the story only. Words land on the beat of the narration if there is one.

Each recipe below is a short build brief. The example clip named in the title is the visual target: open it before building. The chai film (kettle pours into a clay cup, steam rises, 5 s) is the demo story; replace it with the creator's story using `our-guide.md`.

## 39 Memphis diorama

- Example: `examples/39-memphis-diorama.mp4`
- Look: 80s Memphis design: loud patterns, squiggles and a checkered floor in a little room.
- Palette: Pink, teal, yellow and black on white, squiggles and confetti
- Motion rules: Shapes pop in with overshoot, patterns scroll slowly, the checkered floor holds still.
- Typography: Bold geometric sans.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Youth brands, retail, playful promos
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 67

## 51 Sticker explainer

- Example: `examples/51-sticker-explainer.mp4`
- Look: Die-cut stickers with white borders that pop in, with bold word stickers.
- Palette: Bright flats, 6 px white die-cut border, soft shadow
- Motion rules: Stickers pop in with scale overshoot and a 3 degree tilt, word stickers land on the beat.
- Typography: Rounded heavy sans, 1 to 2 words per sticker.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG plus DOM text)
- Best for: Social explainers, steps, listicles
- Also see: none

## 52 Math diagram

- Example: `examples/52-math-diagram.mp4`
- Look: A chalk-on-navy maths explainer: angles, points, a dotted pour path and a fill graph.
- Palette: Navy #0f1f4a, chalk white, accent cyan #26c9d5 and amber
- Motion rules: Points and arcs draw on in order, labels fade after their object, a graph traces the fill.
- Typography: Italic serif for symbols, sans for words.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Maths, physics, data, STEM lessons
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 09; manim-composer; code-motion-styles 02
