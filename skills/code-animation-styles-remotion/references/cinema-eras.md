# Cinema eras

Film damage is seeded noise, never Math.random. Grain, flicker and scratches are one overlay group that can be switched off. Intertitles and headlines are short English about the creator's story.

Each recipe below is a short build brief. The example clip named in the title is the visual target: open it before building. The chai film (kettle pours into a clay cup, steam rises, 5 s) is the demo story; replace it with the creator's story using `our-guide.md`.

## 32 Silent film

- Example: `examples/32-silent-film.mp4`
- Look: Black and white 1920s film: grain, flicker, scratches and a stage curtain.
- Palette: Black, white and greys, vignette
- Motion rules: Frame jitter, flicker 3 to 6 percent, scratches and dust seeded, 20 fps feel, intertitle cards.
- Typography: Serif intertitles, 3 to 6 words.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG plus canvas)
- Best for: Humour, history, nostalgia
- Also see: none (ai-style-atlas 08 for noir)

## 33 Newsreel

- Example: `examples/33-newsreel.mp4`
- Look: Sepia 1940s newsreel with a slanted headline banner and film damage.
- Palette: Sepia #c9a36a on dark brown, cream banner
- Motion rules: Slanted banner slams in, scratches and burn marks seeded, light shake on hits.
- Typography: Bold slab or condensed headline, upper case.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG plus canvas)
- Best for: Announcements, history, news style hooks
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 65
