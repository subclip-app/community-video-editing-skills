# Print and craft

The look is mostly filters and limited inks. Use feTurbulence plus feDisplacementMap for rough edges, mix-blend-mode multiply for ink layers, and keep the palette to the listed inks.

Each recipe below is a short build brief. The example clip named in the title is the visual target: open it before building. The chai film (kettle pours into a clay cup, steam rises, 5 s) is the demo story; replace it with the creator's story using `our-guide.md`.

## 18 Woodcut

- Example: `examples/18-woodcut.mp4`
- Look: Black and white relief print with gouged lines and rough edges.
- Palette: Black #111 on off-white #efe9dc, two colours only
- Motion rules: Rough displacement on every edge, gouge lines drawn on, hold on twos.
- Typography: Bold serif if text is needed.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG with filters)
- Best for: History, folklore, dramatic stories
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 29

## 19 Engraving

- Example: `examples/19-engraving.mp4`
- Look: Fine hatched lines like an old banknote, in a green ornamental frame.
- Palette: Green ink #2f5d3a on cream #f1ead7, ornament frame
- Motion rules: Hatch lines drawn in parallel sets, density gives shade, frame draws on first.
- Typography: Engraved serif, short.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Finance, heritage, official tone
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 81

## 20 Silkscreen poster

- Example: `examples/20-silkscreen-poster.mp4`
- Look: Flat ink layers, sunburst rays and slight misregistration, like a screen-printed poster.
- Palette: 3 to 4 flat inks with multiply blend, sunburst rays
- Motion rules: Layers slide in 2 to 3 px off register, rays rotate slowly, halftone dots optional.
- Typography: Heavy condensed sans for a title.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Events, music, launches
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 80

## 21 Ukiyo-e

- Example: `examples/21-ukiyo-e.mp4`
- Look: Flat colour and ink outlines like a Japanese woodblock print, with clouds and blossom.
- Palette: Indigo, vermilion, cream paper, soft cloud bands
- Motion rules: Flat fills, ink outlines, drifting cloud bands, blossom petals fall on a slow loop.
- Typography: None; a red seal mark is fine.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Travel, tea and food, calm culture pieces
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 21

## 22 Art deco

- Example: `examples/22-art-deco.mp4`
- Look: Gold lines, stepped frames and sunrays on deep teal, like a 1920s poster.
- Palette: Deep teal #0e3b43, gold #d4a64a, cream
- Motion rules: Gold lines draw on with symmetry, sunrays fan out, stepped frames build inward.
- Typography: Geometric deco display font, upper case.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Luxury, hotels, finance, cocktails
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 51

## 23 Stained glass

- Example: `examples/23-stained-glass.mp4`
- Look: Coloured glass panes and black lead lines in an arched window.
- Palette: Jewel colours, lead lines #111, back glow
- Motion rules: Panes light up one by one, light beams drift across, lead lines stay static.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Faith and heritage, ceremony, art
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 25
