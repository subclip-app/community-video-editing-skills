# Cartoon

Shapes carry the look. Keep the character and prop as separate groups so the acting (tilt, squash, pour) is a transform on a group, not a redraw.

Each recipe below is a short build brief. The example clip named in the title is the visual target: open it before building. The chai film (kettle pours into a clay cup, steam rises, 5 s) is the demo story; replace it with the creator's story using `our-guide.md`.

## 11 Flat vector

- Example: `examples/11-flat-vector.mp4`
- Look: Clean 2D flat shapes, bright colours and no outlines, like an explainer app.
- Palette: Pastel background plus 4 to 5 saturated flats
- Motion rules: Smooth eased moves, squash and stretch of 5 to 8 percent, shapes slide and scale with overshoot.
- Typography: Geist Sans or any clean sans for labels.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: SaaS, finance, app explainers
- Also see: ai-style-atlas 01; ai-illustration-styles

## 12 80s cel anime

- Example: `examples/12-cel-anime-80s.mp4`
- Look: Hard cel shading, a sunset sky and a city skyline, like an 80s anime still.
- Palette: Sunset gradient magenta to orange, deep blue skyline, hard shade tone per object
- Motion rules: Hard two-tone shading, speed lines on fast moves, a slow background pan, grain on the whole frame.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Music, gaming, youth culture, launches
- Also see: code-motion-styles 08; ai-illustration-styles

## 13 Stick figure

- Example: `examples/13-stick-figure.mp4`
- Look: A white round-head stick figure pours the chai under one hanging lamp.
- Palette: Dark background #15171c, white figure, one warm lamp cone
- Motion rules: Figure limbs as 2 segment lines, simple IK on the pouring arm, lamp cone flickers never.
- Typography: None.
- Engine: HyperFrames (SVG)
- Best for: Explainers, humour, any abstract process
- Also see: code-motion-styles 07; svg-character-animation
