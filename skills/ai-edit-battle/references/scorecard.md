# Battle scorecard

## Hard checks (pass or fail, per edit)

- Duration equals the raw clip.
- Original voice untouched and in sync.
- No blank, frozen or broken stretch, and a finished ending.
- Text inside safe margins, never over a face, spelled exactly as spoken.
- No missing assets, no leftover draft layers.

An edit that fails a hard check cannot win unless the other fails too.

## Scored categories (1 to 5, one line of evidence each)

| Category | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Concept | One visual idea runs through the film and pays off in the ending. |
| Layout variety | Six or more different layout systems, never the same twice in a row, three hero moments. |
| Motion and transitions | Eased entrances and exits, motivated hand-overs, no stretch of stillness over half a second. |
| Typography | Clear hierarchy, effects spent on the key beats only, readable at half size. |
| Footage integration | Footage and graphics share the space, faces always readable, the speaker's gestures trigger graphics. |
| Sound | Music with structure, effects on the right frames, voice always clear. |
| Polish | No collisions, popping or clipping; the whole thing looks finished. |

## Result sheet

Record per edit: total, hard-check result, time taken, credits used, one best moment and one weakness. Declare a winner only from this sheet, and say when the margin is small. The audience can overrule it.
