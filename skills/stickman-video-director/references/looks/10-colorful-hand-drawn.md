# 10 B2 Colorful hand-drawn

- **Reference clip:** `examples/12-colorful-hand-drawn.mp4` (why trying to sleep keeps you awake). Original author's sample, first 10 s, silent. Visual inspiration credited upstream to a YouTube channel (see README).
- **Upstream source:** `references/b2-colorful-handdrawn.md` in [kaomei/stickman-video-director](https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director/blob/8394bec0148fa281ea59171d90fddc13afb80c4e/skills/directing-stickman-videos/references/b2-colorful-handdrawn.md).
- **Engine:** B, stills plus motion layers. **Best for:** everyday psychology, sleep, habits, attention, relatable life explainers.

## The look

Warm paper textures, soft colors and handwritten touches. Fine irregular ink lines, colorful everyday rooms, ordinary round-headed stickmen with sparse dot eyes and a short line mouth, illustrated metaphors, and ivory paper-label captions in dark ink. Gentle motion, scenic cut-aways, concrete payoffs. Not B1 with a color filter.

## Image recipe

```text
Hand-drawn colorful illustration on warm paper texture. Fine irregular black ink contours with muted but varied colored props and recognizable everyday environments, simple graphic depth with useful negative space. Ordinary round-headed stickman with sparse dot eyes and a short line mouth, [pose, gaze, expression]. Setting: [location and layout, viewpoint]. Props: [owner and state]. No text, no sterile dashboard, no glossy 3D, no black-and-white grid.
```

Pose, gaze, expression and context give identity; no costume or mascot is imposed. Person-free object shots and contextual compositions are welcome where anatomy adds nothing. Keep object contact honest: a hand holding a phone must touch it.

## Scene map

Before drawing a new story, note per shot: narrative job, location and layout, viewpoint, staging (or person-free), and how it differs from the previous shot. Do not default to the same room, door, clock and standing presenter.

## Motion, captions, sound

A HyperFrames layer per scene with small local moves (gaze, a hand, a drifting paper boat, a clock hand). Stable readable poses with small reactions beat constant limb waving. A visible change at least every 2.5 seconds without irrelevant fidgeting. Handwritten scene headings; captions as a compact ivory label in dark clean sans, short semantic phrases (40 design units on a 1280 wide plane for landscape, 72 for portrait). Restrained action effects below the narration and a warm bed. Local voice.

## Subclip route and credits

Same as look 09: stills via Codex CLI, else `nano-banana-2` (2.5 credits each); everything else free.

## Checks

Hand and prop contact; the same drawing language across a changing set; captions as paper labels; no unsupported psychology or medical claims in the script; the picture changes meaningfully, not by decoration.
