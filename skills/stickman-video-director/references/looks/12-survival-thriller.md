# 12 B4 Survival thriller

- **Reference clip:** `examples/14-survival-thriller.mp4` (911 dispatcher rules). Original author's sample, first 10 s of a 92 s film, silent (its narration and music are not reused). Visual inspiration credited upstream to a YouTube channel (see README).
- **Upstream source:** `references/b4-survival-storytelling.md`, `b4-opening-direction.md`, `b4-audio-direction.md`, `b4-picture-animation.md` in [kaomei/stickman-video-director](https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director/blob/8394bec0148fa281ea59171d90fddc13afb80c4e/skills/directing-stickman-videos/references/b4-survival-storytelling.md).
- **Engine:** B, stills plus motion layers. **Best for:** survival rules, urban legends, disaster escapes, suspense with a twist.

## The look

Clear slightly organic black contours, broad painted color fields, restrained cel shadows, detailed but readable full-color 2D environments. A simple round white head, minimal black face, unclothed black stick body and compact rounded white gloves; half-shadowed openings, unnerving close-ups, threats emerging from the dark. Not glossy concept art, volumetric light, heavy bloom or crushed blacks.

## Image recipe

```text
Full-color 2D illustration with clear slightly organic black contours, broad painted color fields and restrained cel shadows, detailed readable environment with foreground, midground and background. Character: simple round white head, minimal black face, bare black stick body and limbs, compact rounded white gloves with a readable thumb, coherent shoulder to elbow to wrist lengths, one pelvis branching into two legs. [Story-specific role, accessory, location, light, camera]. No text in the image, no realistic skin or materials, no volumetric glow.
```

Make every scene story-specific: roles, rooms and props change with the script; a headset, rain or a blue-night palette belongs to one story, not the style. Check hands (continuous wrists, real grip, readable thumb) and legs (one torso into two distinct legs) in every still.

## Opening, shots and sound

First three seconds escalate visually and give a hook (a distinct new view, not a cropped later still). Every shot lasts at most three seconds, and meaningful action fits inside that. Use local picture animation, not only a crop or zoom. Sound: a narrator voice (a deep male voice suits the original), a quiet continuous ambient bed, story-synced effects that stay under the voice, a cliffhanger choice at the end. If the lead role is female, use a female narrator.

## Typography

Quieter rounded setup text, narrow heavy poster-style payoff with a clear primary and supporting line, warm cream with a coral emphasis, controlled offset depth. Build it as live HTML text in a layer, grouped by meaning.

## Subclip route and credits

Stills via Codex CLI, else `nano-banana-2` (2.5 credits each). A reference sheet in the look makes later stills match. HyperFrames layers for local animation; local voice and effects are free.

## Checks

White round head and gloves intact; rooms differ by story; no shot over three seconds; the horror stays readable (not too dark); opening differs from the ending; narration and effects balance set by volume and listened to by the creator.
