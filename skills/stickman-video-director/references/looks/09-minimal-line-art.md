# 09 B1 Minimal line art

- **Reference clip:** `examples/11-minimal-line-art.mp4` (YouTube without showing your face). Original author's sample, first 10 s of a 16 s film, silent.
- **Upstream source:** `references/b1-minimal-handdrawn.md`, `b1-b2-b3-native-portrait.md` in [kaomei/stickman-video-director](https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director/blob/8394bec0148fa281ea59171d90fddc13afb80c4e/skills/directing-stickman-videos/references/b1-minimal-handdrawn.md).
- **Engine:** B, stills plus motion layers. **Best for:** YouTube explainers, education, psychology, personal growth, workplace stories, product intros. The cheapest look to run.

## The look

Like a friend sketching an idea while explaining. Near-white warm paper with an extremely faint broad square grid, expressive hand-drawn stick figures in fine slightly irregular black pen, hand-colored props, marker accents and curved arrows, large short scene text as the focal point, limited warm yellow, red and green plus ink navy. Expressions, props and subtle motion carry the idea.

## Image recipe (one still per shot, same lock every time)

```text
Hand-drawn explainer sketch on near-white warm paper with an extremely faint broad square grid. Slender stick figure, about 4 to 4.5 heads tall: a modest round head, a long single-line torso and limbs with fine slightly irregular black pen outlines, small oval hands and feet, no filled or triangular torso, no mitten hands. Strongly readable expression: black oval eyes, visibly raised or pinched brows, a mouth that clearly shows [emotion]. Whole-body acting: gaze, head tilt, lean, reach and weight-bearing legs agree with the emotion; hands genuinely touch [prop]. Hand-colored recognizable props in [warm yellow, red, green], marker accents and curved arrows. Large short hand-lettered text is NOT drawn into this image (blank paper area left for it). Shot: [composition, viewpoint]. Leave the bottom band empty for captions.
```

Make a small story-specific batch (3 to 6 poses, expressions and props) before composing; do not recycle one pose across unrelated lines. A shot may be person-free: a close-up of an object or diagram. Keep one clear focal action per frame.

## Motion in a HyperFrames layer

One layer per scene, built over the still: head and hand moves, a prop carried between ideas, handwritten words that build in order (vary the order by meaning; sometimes show the whole composition at once), arrows that draw themselves, a settled frame after an action. Do not apply the same title, icon, underline build to every shot, and do not keep the camera moving. Characters fill about 60 to 70 percent of direct screen time; include character-free close-ups and reading holds.

Text: large hand-lettered phrases drawn as live HTML text in a handwriting font (a free handwriting font such as Caveat is a safe choice), captions as a short phrase line at the bottom in a clean semi-bold sans.

## Portrait (9:16)

Re-compose, do not crop: bigger captions inside the safe band, a per-shot focal region, vertical reading order, and a phone-size check at 1x.

## Sound and voice

Native-speed voice leads. Soft bed, pencil and paper effects. Local voice, gender matched to the figure.

## Subclip route and credits

Stills from the creator's Codex CLI first (the original used gpt-image-2), else `nano-banana-2` at 2.5 credits each; local voice, captions and layers are free. A 20 to 30 second film is about 6 to 12 stills, so 0 credits via Codex or about 15 to 30 on `nano-banana-2`. Quote it.

## Checks

Fine pen line and consistent figure; expressions pushed far enough to read at small size; hands touch props; text large, short and the focal point; not every shot builds the same way; captions in the lower band.
