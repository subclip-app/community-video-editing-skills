# 14 B6 Meme story animation

- **Reference clip:** `examples/16-meme-story-animation.mp4` (1990s Hong Kong, buns and arcades). Original author's sample, first 10 s of a 20 s film, silent. Visual inspiration credited upstream to a YouTube channel (see README).
- **Upstream source:** `references/b6-dynamic-meme.md`, `b6-art-direction.md`, `b6-character-binding.md`, `b6-native-formats.md` in [kaomei/stickman-video-director](https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director/blob/8394bec0148fa281ea59171d90fddc13afb80c4e/skills/directing-stickman-videos/references/b6-dynamic-meme.md).
- **Engine:** B, painted cut-outs plus motion layers. **Best for:** historical oddities, con stories, absurd comedy, short twist stories. Works best in portrait (9:16). The hardest look to get right: say so to the creator.

## The look

Expressive meme faces, loose hand-drawn ink lines of varying weight, matte paint and soft light across wide layered environments. Heads, bodies and scenery share one hand-drawn treatment. Limbs are readable single-stroke pressure-varying ink arms with small dark hands. Wide scenes give characters, props and camera moves room: lateral tracking, layered depth, handoffs, reaction close-ups, clear pauses for each visual punchline.

## Image recipe

```text
Hand-painted [era] [place] scene, matte colored masses, soft light, loose varying-weight ink contours, a few visible brush boundaries, wide layered environment with foreground, midground and background. Character: [meme-style expressive head, slightly crooked clothed torso in the era's clothing, thin dark ink arms with small dark hands and simple shoes]; the head and body share the same ink pressure and paint softness. No uniform zigzags, no vector-perfect lines, no polished realistic fashion, no text.
```

For a period or place, save a short look brief first: year range, silhouettes, hairstyle, accessories, palette, architecture. Era design must not turn the cast into polished realistic humans.

## Parts and build

Generate once and cut out: head views (front, side, back), body, limbs as separate ink strokes, hands and shoes, wide background plates, prop parts. Bind them in a HyperFrames layer (pivots at shoulder, elbow, hip; head on neck), plan each beat as cause, action, reaction and payoff, and time every move to the voice. Check complete limb chains, fixed segment lengths, elbow direction, occlusion, handoffs and grounded feet. Use a low-resolution draft first, then repair only the broken ranges.

## Captions and sound

Heavy white caption type with a dark outline, phrase by phrase, placed inside the safe band (for 9:16 about 47 pixels at 720 x 1280). Bouncy bed and punchy effects. An energetic, cheerful voice, gender matched to the narrator persona.

## Subclip route and credits

A character part set and 3 to 4 wide plates: roughly 8 to 14 stills (free via Codex CLI, or 20 to 35 credits on `nano-banana-2`). Layers and voice are free. Time cost is high, not credits.

## Checks

One consistent ink and paint grammar; limb weight matches across the cast; no floating or stretched joints; each beat has contact and a reaction; the camera move serves the story. Be honest in the report if the puppetry is rough: this is the least automatic look.
