# Background swap with no green screen

Goal: the same person, lips and gestures in a new place. Run it on its own, before any relight.

## Prepare

Trim to the exact moment. Keep the shot as filmed. Read the real objects in the room from the clip (walls, sofa, TV, shelves, lamps) and write them down. You will name them in REMOVE.

## The blocks

| Block | What to write |
|---|---|
| Keep | The face, hair, every movement, gesture, expression and word, camera position and framing, frame for frame. Everything not listed below stays exactly as in the clip. |
| The person | Face 100% matching the clip, the same clothes and accessories listed by name. |
| Remove | The room: list its real objects and its indoor lamp light. |
| Background | The new place with real scale and depth: what is near, what is far, haze on the distance, the horizon, sky and sea or street. Say how many metres the near object stands behind the person. |
| Light | A source that matches the new place (late-morning sun from frame left), the effect on the face, a gentle sky fill, a natural shadow under the chin. Natural colour, true whites, no orange cast unless you want it. |
| Look | The same real camera as the clip: same sharpness, the background slightly softer with natural depth of field, no bloom, flare or CG sheen. |
| Audio | Keep the original audio on its own item, speech in sync. |
| Lock | No other people, no added text. |

## Example places that work

A sunlit stone terrace on a coast with a village on the hill and a lemon tree in a pot; a rain-soaked neon street; a quiet library; a studio set. Choose a place with one clear light direction.

## Then

Compare duration, lips and the edge of hair and hands. If the edge flickers, run once more with a calmer background or a larger gap between person and background. Then you can relight the result in a second pass.
