# Warm Grain (overlay) (frame-warm-grain)

Best for: Product launch or lifestyle brand intro, magazine-style stat moments over footage.

## Canvas and engine

HyperFrames transparent layer over footage, 16:9, 10 to 14 s.

## Look

Palette: Cream #F5F0E0, forest green #3B5E3A, ochre #CC8832, terracotta #C45D3E, brown caption box #7A6248.

Type: Outfit, rounded humanist sans.

## Layers

- Speaker or title card: a forest green card sliding from the left with a cream title and ochre subtitle.
- Three stat shapes, one per moment: a green circle, an ochre pill, a terracotta rounded rectangle with a blob icon.
- A light grain wash (noise image) over the footage.

## Timeline beats

Build these as tweens on one paused timeline. Times are in seconds from the start of the layer.

- t=0.2 title card slides in over 0.8 s (power2.out); exits at t=1.8 over 0.6 s.
- Moment 1 at t=1.8: drifts 40 px into place with scale 0.8 to 1 over 1.2 s (back.out), fades at t=4.2.
- Moment 2 at t=4.6: drifts down 40 px into place, fades at t=8.3.
- Moment 3 at t=8.8: drifts in from the right, fades at t=13.5.
- Captions on the brown box appear per line and hide 0.1 s after the line ends.

## Signature to keep

Soft, hand-cut shapes in earth tones that drift rather than snap.

## Notes for Subclip

Use the grain image at low opacity on a layer with no text. Retime the three moments to real speech.

Follow [layer-rules.md](layer-rules.md) for seekable timing, export-safe styling and fonts. Replace every sample word and number with the creator's real content.
