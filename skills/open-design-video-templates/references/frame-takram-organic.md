# Takram Organic (frame-takram-organic)

Best for: System or architecture concept reveal, warm human product story, network or memory graph explainer.

## Canvas and engine

HyperFrames, 16:9, 5 s of motion then a calm hold, about 8 s total.

## Look

Palette: Beige #EFEAE0, card cream at 66 percent, text #2E2E28, sage #7A9E7F, terracotta #C98A5E, grey #8A867C.

Type: Manrope: 700 at 92 px for the title, 600 uppercase for the eyebrow, 400 for the caption.

## Layers

- Soft sage radial wash on the right.
- Frosted rounded card (760 by 800 px, 40 px radius) on the left holding eyebrow, two-line title with one terracotta word, and a caption.
- A radial graph on the right: a large terracotta center node (40 px radius) and eight sage nodes (22 px) at 230 px from the center, joined by thin sage links.

## Timeline beats

Build these as tweens on one paused timeline. Times are in seconds from the start of the layer.

- t=0.2 card fades up 26 px and scales from 0.985 over 1 s (expo.out).
- t=0.7 eyebrow fades up; t=0.9 title; t=1.2 caption.
- t=1.3 center node pops (back.out 1.7, 0.55 s).
- t=1.4 to 1.96 eight links draw outward from the center, 0.08 s apart, 1.1 s each.
- t=1.9 to 2.46 eight nodes pop 0.08 s apart.
- From t=2 the graph floats up and down 4 percent over a 7 s sine, then returns to rest.

## Signature to keep

Links draw first, then nodes pop outward, then the whole graph breathes.

## Notes for Subclip

Frosted glass is not export-safe; use a solid cream card at 90 percent opacity. Replace the sample node count and add small labels beside each node if the creator names the concepts.

Follow [layer-rules.md](layer-rules.md) for seekable timing, export-safe styling and fonts. Replace every sample word and number with the creator's real content.
