# Product Promo (20 s) (frame-product-promo)

Best for: Product showcase, multi-feature reel, hero promo with a logo intro and outro.

## Canvas and engine

HyperFrames, 16:9, 20 s: intro 1.5 s, middle 12.5 s, outro 6 s. Build as three scene groups in one layer.

## Look

Palette: Dark canvas with a dot grid, brand colors supplied by the creator. The sample used a multi-color logo and a light UI frame.

Type: A neutral grotesque for UI text and a heavier face for the hero heading.

## Layers

- Scene 1: the logo assembles from five pieces with an expanding ring.
- Scenes 2 to 4: a dot-grid canvas holding a web frame that builds itself: nav bar, logo, three links, hero heading, subtitle lines and a button, with a cursor that edits it.
- Scene 5: logo again with a glow, a tagline and a URL pill.

## Timeline beats

Build these as tweens on one paused timeline. Times are in seconds from the start of the layer.

- t=0 to 1.5 logo pieces assemble and a ring expands once.
- t=1.5 to 14 the web frame scales in, the heading types in, lines draw, the button pops, the cursor moves between elements, the layout changes once or twice.
- t=14 to 20 logo returns with a glow, tagline rises at t=15, URL pill pops at t=16, hold to the end.

## Signature to keep

Show the product by building its interface on screen.

## Notes for Subclip

Use the creator's real product screenshots or a simplified UI of their product, and their own logo. Do not copy any other company's logo or UI; the original sample imitates a third-party brand and its pieces must be replaced.

Follow [layer-rules.md](layer-rules.md) for seekable timing, export-safe styling and fonts. Replace every sample word and number with the creator's real content.
