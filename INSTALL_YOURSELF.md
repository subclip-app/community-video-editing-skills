## How to install it yourself in Subclip

Open the **Skills** window, choose **Add skill**, and paste the original GitHub link above (click a preview to open it). The app scans it and your own agent reviews it before you confirm installation. Follow the original author's license and attribution terms, including any limits on commercial use.
